# NightWatch

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
NightWatch is a private, single-operator infrastructure console. Its public showcase primarily serves builders and recruiters who want to understand the product, its engineering, and its creator's work.

## Product Purpose
Unify infrastructure observations and turn natural-language intent into reviewable, approval-bound operations with preserved evidence.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The existing product includes a 3D/2D infrastructure world, multi-source reconciliation, freshness-aware telemetry, incident timelines, persistent agent conversations, deployment workflows, typed Dokploy operations, versioned approvals, stale-state checks, and operational reports. The frontend, backend, and Docker observer are separate services. Integration credentials stay on the server. Monitoring does not automatically launch paid model investigations.

## Brand Commitments
The public page is a cinematic showcase, with the approved Infrastructure Theatre direction: sculptural floating islands, charcoal and mint, readable pauses, and one scroll-driven incident story. Operator tools remain private. The creator's name is Midhun P M.

## Evidence on Hand
README.md and docs/ describe the implementation. Six product screenshots and three hackathon media assets (a builders photograph, badge photograph, and results graphic) are available. The repository records Second Prize at Codex Community Hackathon Calicut, held at TinkerSpace on 19–20 September 2026. A narrated walkthrough is linked in README.md. Public scene data and the incident animation are representative simulations, not live infrastructure observations.

## Product Principles
- Keep observations, hypotheses, approval, execution, and verification distinct.
- Make technical depth discoverable without requiring operator credentials.
- Preserve unavailable and stale evidence rather than inventing measurements.
- Public exploration must not invoke private infrastructure or paid models.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
The complete public narrative is readable without JavaScript, WebGL, or animation. Mobile and reduced-motion visitors receive a linear story and scene poster. All controls support keyboard operation.
